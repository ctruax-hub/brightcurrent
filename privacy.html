Privacy Policy

Demo content for a fictional brand. Last updated October 2026.

What we collect

When you chat with us we collect the messages you send, the time of the conversation, and basic technical details such as your browser type. If you give us your name, email or order number, we store those too.

How we use it

We use this information to answer your questions, look up orders, improve our service, and connect you to a human representative if you ask.

Sharing

We share chat data only with service providers that help us run the chat, and with the Hallworth & Pierce Company family of brands where needed to support you.

Your choices

You can ask us to access or delete your chat history through the chat assistant or the Contact Us link.
